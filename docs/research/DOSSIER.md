## 1. The Read (one page, plain language)

Here is the market as the evidence actually shows it.

Tampa Bay criminal defense is not one race, it is several, and they are not
equally winnable. The obvious head terms, "criminal defense attorney Tampa" and
"federal criminal defense attorney Tampa," are behind a wall. The top two
organic slots are legal directories (Justia, Super Lawyers), and the rest belong
to firms that have been publishing for a decade or more, plus one national player
(Oberheiden) that has automated its way onto six of nine federal keywords. A new
firm does not move those in a year, and it should not spend a dollar or an hour
trying to (competitors.md, keywords.md). Chasing them is the single most common
way firms in your position waste a marketing budget.

But the market has a hole in the middle of it, and it is your hole. Nobody owns
Brandon. Not one of the seven competitors we profiled in depth has a Brandon
office, a Brandon page, or a Brandon Google Business Profile. The only
Brandon-based criminal firm we found anywhere is Curry Law Group, and Curry
spends all of its advertising on personal injury and family law, none on
criminal defense (competitors.md, paid-reality.md). The same is true one town
over in Riverview, in Plant City, and in Wesley Chapel. These are real, distinct
search markets with weak incumbents, and your physical location gives you a
proximity advantage in them that no downtown Tampa firm can copy
(keywords.md Section 5).

There are two more open doors. First, content: the firm that actually writes the
best explainers in this market is Sammis Law Firm, not any of the big-name
competitors, and even Sammis leaves whole subjects thin or dated. There is no
good Middle District of Florida federal-process guide from any private Tampa
firm, no annotated federal target-letter walkthrough anywhere in the country, no
immigration-consequences guide that covers the one case (Padilla) a noncitizen
defendant most needs, and the best local sentencing-guidelines page was last
updated in February 2022 (content-gaps.md). Second, paid advertising: among the
thirteen firms we checked in Google's Ads Transparency Center, essentially
nobody advertises criminal defense here. Rankin and Brunvand run zero Google
ads. Only one firm runs criminal search ads at all, and nobody advertises
criminal defense in Brandon in any channel we could observe (paid-reality.md).
That is a rare thing: an uncontested paid channel in a high-value practice area.

So the thesis is simple. Do not fight the head-term and federal-brand wars in
year one. Own Brandon and the towns around it. Fill the informational gaps
nobody fills, with pages and tools built to a standard the current market does
not meet. And once your review count and phone coverage can support it, walk
into the paid vacuum that your competitors have left wide open.

**Three decisions only you can make, and they gate the plan** (all from
STATE.md open questions):

1. **Will the firm have a real, Google-eligible physical office address in
   Brandon?** The entire local strategy, the map pack, and Local Services Ads
   all depend on this. If the answer is no, the plan changes materially.
2. **What is the year-one practice mix, state volume or federal/white-collar?**
   The plan below recommends conceding the federal and white-collar head-term
   fights in year one and building federal *content* for the long game. Confirm
   that matches your intent.
3. **Is there a paid budget, and does the firm have 24/7 call answering and
   professional liability insurance?** The paid vacuum is the biggest single
   opportunity we found, but capturing it requires spend, a phone that is always
   answered (it drives Local Services Ads ranking), and insurance (a screening
   prerequisite). Without these, paid waits.

**What winning looks like at month 12** (inferred, grounded in the benchmarks
below): a claimed and optimized Google Business Profile at 50 to 100 reviews and
a 4.9-plus rating, ranking in the Brandon, Riverview, and Plant City map results;
the best sentencing-scoresheet tool and the best Middle District federal guides
in the market; a Local Services Ads presence no local competitor contests; and a
content library that captures the NOW-graded and 6-to-12-month keywords while the
directory-walled head terms are deliberately left alone.

## 2. The 12-Month Plan (priority order)

Every action cites the file that justifies it. Gated actions say what they wait
for.

### Months 1-3: Foundation (own Brandon, start the flywheels)

1. **Stand up the Google Business Profile now, correctly.** Primary category
   "Criminal justice attorney," Brandon address, full hours, attributes, photos.
   This is the highest-leverage local asset and it is empty in the market
   (local-authority.md Section 5). Gated on Decision 1 (real Brandon address).
2. **Start the review program on day one.** Ask every closed-case client, no
   incentives, target 4 to 8 reviews per month (local-authority.md Section 2;
   Rules 4-7.13(b)(9) and 4-7.17(b) permit asking, prohibit incentives). This is
   the slowest-compounding asset, so it must start first, and it is the gate for
   Local Services Ads later.
3. **Claim the SERP-visible directory profiles, free tier only:** Justia, Super
   Lawyers, LawInfo, FindLaw (Brandon), and the Spanish directories abogado.com
   and elAbogado if the Spanish play is live. Skip paid placement on Avvo,
   Martindale, Lawyers.com, and Expertise.com (local-authority.md Section 3;
   avoid.md Section 3).
4. **Build the Brandon, Riverview, and Plant City location content** with honest
   locality (the firm actually sits in Brandon), courthouse framing for the Plant
   City / east-Hillsborough angle (keywords.md Section 5). Never imply an office
   that does not exist (Rule 4-7.12; avoid.md item 24).
5. **Begin the two anchor content clusters** (see the 15-page list, Section 4):
   the sentencing hub plus calculator, and the Middle District federal hub plus
   timeline. These combine NOW-graded keywords with the two widest verified
   content gaps (content-gaps.md Section 3 build note).
6. **Set up the local-citation plumbing:** Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, BBB,
   data aggregators, consistent NAP (local-authority.md Section 5).
7. **Join and get listed:** Hillsborough County Bar Association, FACDL and its
   local chapter (HCACDL), NACDL, the merged Brandon Riverview Chamber, Bay Area
   VLP (local-authority.md Section 4).

### Months 4-6: Depth and the first tools

8. **Ship tool #1, the FL CPC scoresheet calculator with charge-lookup input,**
   built to the October 23, 2025 Rule 3.992 revision, Hillsborough-framed, with
   the level 1-10 spoke pages (tools-and-hooks.md Section 3; content-gaps.md
   pages 1-3). Market it best-in-state, never "first in Florida" (avoid.md item
   14).
9. **Ship tool #2, the Hillsborough First-24-Hours interactive checklist,**
   absorbing the state bond walkthrough, targeting the county's best conversion
   query (tools-and-hooks.md Section 3). Verify the bond-schedule and
   Administrative Order S-2024-030 amounts against the primary source before
   publishing (content-gaps.md page 14, STATE.md).
10. **Build out the federal pre-indictment content cluster** (target letters
    with annotated sample, grand jury subpoena, proffer, detention hearings, FBI
    contact, device seizure). Highest case value per visit, and several are
    open fields nationally (content-gaps.md pages 6-11).
11. **Launch Local Services Ads, gated on ~35+ reviews and 24/7 answering.**
    This is the entry into the paid vacuum; do not launch it before the gate
    (paid-reality.md Sections 3.5 and 4; avoid.md item 32). Gated on Decision 3.
12. **Begin a small Brandon-geofenced search campaign** on emergency, DUI, and
    drug-possession terms, call-tracked, as much to buy real Tampa cost data as
    leads (paid-reality.md Section 4.3). Gated on Decision 3.

### Months 7-12: Compound and expand

13. **Ship tool #3, the MDFL Tampa Division federal case timeline** (visual),
    feeding the federal funnel (tools-and-hooks.md Section 3).
14. **Complete the state process and collateral-consequences cluster:** VOP, PTI
    and diversion, professional-license consequences, immigration consequences
    with Padilla (content-gaps.md pages 15-18). The license and immigration pages
    convert high-value white-collar and noncitizen clients.
15. **Scale paid from observed data,** moving toward the medium scenario only if
    small-scenario numbers validate the Tampa bands; add the capped
    federal-moment search experiment (paid-reality.md Section 4.3).
16. **Deprioritized, build last if at all:** the sealing/expungement page,
    Brandon-framed (content-gaps.md page 19; avoid.md item 29).
17. **Spanish content, only if genuine Spanish capability is confirmed:** a
    small set of Spanish pages mirroring the firm's differentiators, not a
    full-site translation (keywords.md Section 6). Gated on Decision from
    STATE.md Q8.

## 3. Review-Velocity Target (the number and its math)

**Target: 50 to 100 Google reviews at a 4.9-plus rating within 12 months, via a
4-to-8-review-per-month ask-every-closed-case program** (local-authority.md
Section 2).

The math, from the observed benchmark ladder (4 counts Maps-verified, the rest
aggregator-reported with a 5-to-25-percent staleness band):

- 2 reviews/month clears the client-named firms' Google profiles (Rankin 10,
  Brunvand Tampa 21, Whittel Tampa 15).
- 3 reviews/month clears Brancato (~34, observed accumulating at ~1.9/month).
- 11 reviews/month would be needed to catch Curry Law Group (131), the Brandon
  incumbent, within the year.
- 14 to 17 reviews/month would chase the pack leaders (Smith & Eulo 193).

The recommendation lands at 4 to 8 per month because that clears every
client-named competitor comfortably, closes on Brancato and Curry over the year,
and is achievable from real closed-case volume without incentives (which the
Bar prohibits). Reviews are also the gate for Local Services Ads, which is why
the program starts in month 1 (paid-reality.md Section 3.5).

From tools-and-hooks.md Section 3:

   Effort M.

   open nationally, no MDFL incumbent, feeds the high-value federal funnel.
   Effort S-M.

   bail-bondsman intent Google restricts; the attorney slice folds into tool #2.

payoff, highest accuracy risk (tools-and-hooks.md Section 3; avoid.md item 30).

no-legal-advice and no-outcome-guarantee disclaimer adjacent to the output
(tools-and-hooks.md Section 4; avoid.md items 15-16).

## 6. Paid Budget Recommendation (all figures ESTIMATE)

All figures are ESTIMATE; none was observed from a live Tampa auction. Both
scenarios assume the Brandon office (Decision 1) and 24/7 answering (Decision 3)
exist; without them, run neither (paid-reality.md Section 4.3).

- **Small scenario, ~$2,500-3,500/month (recommended start):** Local Services Ads
  $1,500-2,000/month (at $100-200/lead ESTIMATE, roughly 8-18 leads/month, 1-3
  signed cases); Brandon-geofenced exact/phrase search $1,000-1,500/month on
  emergency, DUI, and drug-possession terms. The first 60-90 days buy the firm's
  own Tampa cost data as much as leads, replacing every estimate in this dossier.
- **Medium scenario, ~$6,000-8,000/month:** only after small-scenario data
  validates the bands. Raises LSA bid targets once reviews (~35+) and
  responsiveness are established, widens DUI/drug coverage, adds a capped
  (~$500/month) federal-moment search experiment, and reserves $500-1,000/month
  for landing pages and intake.
- **Expected all-in cost per signed case: roughly $1,000-4,000 (ESTIMATE),** with
  the small scenario aiming at the lower half given Brandon's suburban auction
  profile.

Why paid at all, when the organic leaders skip it: because the vacuum is
verified and real (only one of thirteen firms runs criminal search ads, none in
Brandon), and Local Services Ads ranking is driven by reviews and call
responsiveness, both of which the firm is building anyway (paid-reality.md
Sections 3.4, 3.5, 4). Where organic must carry it instead: the directory-walled
Tampa head terms, the informational long-tail, and the white-collar cluster
(paid-reality.md Section 4.2; avoid.md items 33-34).

Ad-copy compliance: no expert/specialist/award language absent board
certification, required firm name and bona fide office location per Rule 4-7.12,
no implied office the firm does not have (paid-reality.md Section 5; avoid.md
items 17, 24). Criminal defense ads are permitted (the Google bail-bonds ban does
not apply to legal services) but sit in a sensitive category that blocks
advertiser-curated audiences for personalized ads (avoid.md Section 5).

## 10. Addendum, 2026-08-13: the white-collar supply gap

Added after this dossier was finalized. Recorded as an addendum rather than
edited into the body above, because this file is a dated research artifact and
Sections 1 through 9 remain accurate for the evidence available on 2026-07-22.
Source: the wave 1 and wave 2 content briefs in docs/research/briefs/, all SERPs
Tampa-geolocated and verified 2026-08-13.

**The finding.** Across three of three white-collar charge queries, Google is
filling the SERP with documents that are not about the charge:

| Query | What is padding the SERP |
|---|---|
| embezzlement lawyer Tampa | Four of the top ten are competitor **homepages** (Hanlon, Sammis, Scriven, Greater Tampa Law). Only four of twelve results are about embezzlement at all. |
| money laundering defense attorney Tampa | Two of the top five are generic **white-collar hubs** rather than money laundering pages. Goldman Wetzel's **St. Petersburg** page holds #5 on a Tampa query. |
| identity theft defense lawyer Tampa | Hanlon's **St. Petersburg** page holds #5; Joffe ranks at #11 from a **/naples/** URL path. |
| wire fraud attorney Tampa | Mayberry and Trombley & Hanes rank at #7 and #8 with **bank fraud** pages. Only two of the top eight are dedicated wire fraud pages. |

**Why it matters to the market read in Section 1.** This dossier identified two
open doors, Brandon geography and informational content. This is a third and it
is narrower than it first appears: there is a relevance vacuum in the Tampa
white-collar SERPs that a dedicated, genuinely local page could fill.

**Why it is not a third pillar.** Two of the four patterns above are
out-of-market pages ranking on a Tampa query, and that signal cuts the other
way. It means the "Tampa" token is doing little ranking work on those queries and
Google is falling back on domain authority, which is precisely the advantage a
new Brandon domain does not have. Meanwhile the volume data says these SERPs are
close to unloaded: Google Ads reports no measurable Tampa DMA volume for money
laundering, wire fraud, or identity theft attorney terms, on a tool that proved
willing to report 10 per month for embezzlement.

**The recommendation, which lives in the section plan rather than here:** do not
reorder wave 2 to build the white-collar pages first. Ease of ranking is cheap;
demand is scarce; sequence against the scarce input. Full reasoning, the
recommended order, and the four things that would change it are in
docs/build/CASES-WE-DEFEND-PLAN.md Section 7.3.

**Two other corrections from the same brief work**, recorded here because they
bear directly on Sections 3 and 6 above and are set out in full in
docs/build/DECISIONS.md D6, D8 and D9:

- **The review ladder in Section 3 is understated by roughly three times.** It
  topped at Smith & Eulo 193, all aggregator-reported. The Tampa local pack was
  directly observed on 2026-08-13 and its leader, Daniel J. Fernandez, carries
  574 reviews. Local pack entry is a multi-year objective.
- **The first observed Tampa CPC datum.** `criminal defense attorney Tampa`,
  2,400 searches per month, top-of-page bids $31.11 to $97.54. Every CPC figure
  in Section 6 is ESTIMATE. This one is not, and it sits above the national
  keyword-level band W6 called defensible.
