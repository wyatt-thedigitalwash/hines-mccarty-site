# Competitor Dossier: Criminal Defense, Brandon FL and Greater Tampa Bay

Workstream W1 of the market dossier for Hines & McCarty Law. Data gathered 2026-07-22 by research agents in a single session. This file covers competitor site architecture, content depth, reviews, directories, paid media, differentiators, Florida Bar advertising flags, SERP ownership across three keyword clusters, and a sober 12-month beatability read per firm.

---

## 1. Method and Limitations

**How this data was gathered.** Research agents fetched competitor sitemaps and pages directly (browser sessions where Cloudflare blocked plain fetches), pulled Google Maps place panels, queried the Google Ads Transparency Center, and ran keyword SERPs for three clusters. Every claim in this file carries one of four confidence labels:

- **VERIFIED**: directly fetched, screenshotted, or grep-counted this session.
- **REPORTED**: seen only in a SERP snippet, aggregator page, or third-party summary; not independently fetched.
- **ESTIMATE**: a number not directly counted (word counts eyeballed from fetched copy, review-volume guesses); basis stated each time.
- **INFERRED**: a judgment call from the evidence, not an observation. All beatability verdicts are INFERRED.

**Known limitations, stated up front:**

1. **No Tampa-geolocated SERP.** The search tooling does not reproduce a Tampa or Brandon geolocated Google results page. Local pack composition, map ranks, LSA units, and sponsored slots were NOT directly observable for any keyword. Where local pack data appears below it is proxy data (aggregators, Avvo/Birdeye counts) and is labeled as such. This is the single largest gap in the dossier; a manual Tampa-IP or geo-emulated check is recommended before media buying decisions.
2. **Google Maps review counts are location-independent**, so Maps place panels that were loaded in a real browser (Rankin, Brunvand both offices, Whittel & Melton both offices) are treated as VERIFIED even from a non-Tampa session.
3. **Ads Transparency Center is JS-rendered.** Browser-verified results exist for rankinlawoffice.com (0 ads), acquitter.com (0 ads), and thefllawfirm.com (3 LSA-format ads). For scrivenlawfirm.com, criminalattorneytampa.net, tampacriminalattorneys.com, and brancatolawfirm.com the tool returned only a JS shell; ads status for those four firms is INCONCLUSIVE, not a confirmed negative.
4. **Schema markup (JSON-LD) was not inspected on any competitor page.** Recorded as a uniform gap.
5. Word counts on money pages are ESTIMATES produced by fetch summarizers, not exact counts. Sitemap URL counts are VERIFIED where grep-counted and labeled otherwise.
6. One early Maps query auto-redirected to the wrong firm (Brunvand Wise instead of Rankin); the bad figure was caught and discarded after screenshot verification. Treat any un-screenshotted Maps figure with suspicion.
