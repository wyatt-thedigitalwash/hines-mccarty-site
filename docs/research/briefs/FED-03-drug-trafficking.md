element difference: Florida reaches knowing possession at a weight, while

## 5. The C9 differentiator

Ready to paste into `CHARGE_C9` in `src/lib/site.ts` under the key
`federal/drug-trafficking`:

```
statute:   "21 U.S.C. 841"
section:   "Intent to distribute, and what quantity actually does"
assertion: "Quantity sets the mandatory minimum but never the offense: 841(a)(1) turns on intent to distribute, and 841(b)(1)(C) reaches a schedule I or II controlled substance in any amount at all, with a 20 year maximum and no minimum."
```

**Why it is false or nonsensical for any other charge.** The assertion names a
controlled-substance schedule, an intent-to-distribute element, and a penalty
subparagraph that operates with no quantity term. Swap in any other charge in
either silo and at least two of the three become false:

- **Federal gun offences, 922(g).** No schedule, no quantity, no distribution
  intent. The sentence cannot be spoken about it.
- **Federal money laundering, 1956 and 1957.** FED-01's assertion rests on a
  finite predicate list and the presence or absence of a dollar threshold. That
  is about what the money came from. This is about what the defendant meant to
  do with a substance. Opposite subject matter, and neither statute mentions the
  other's operative concept.
- **Florida trafficking, 893.135, the counterpart page.** Here it inverts rather
  than merely failing. In Florida weight is definitional: below the threshold
  there is no trafficking offence at all, and above it there is one regardless of
  intent. The assertion says the exact reverse of the state page's assertion,
  which is the strongest possible outcome for an overlap pair.

**Collision check, by hand.** `statuteKey` resolves this to `21 usc 841`. No
other entry in `CHARGE_C9` uses it, so the automated checker will never compare
this assertion to the state page's, which means the hand check is the real one.
Compared against all nine populated assertions in `src/lib/site.ts` as of
2026-08-13: `state/drug-trafficking-defense` is the only near neighbour and it
rests on substance-specific weight tiers each tied to a fixed mandatory minimum
and fine, with no tier below offense level 7. This one rests on an intent
element and a tier with no quantity in it. **No collision. They contradict each
other by design, which is what OVERLAP-AUDIT.md pair 1 concluded they should.**
