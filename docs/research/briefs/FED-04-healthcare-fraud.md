## 5. The C9 differentiator

Ready to paste into the `c9` field in `src/lib/site.ts`:

```
statute:   "18 U.S.C. 1347"
section:   "Any health care benefit program, and why a private insurer counts"
assertion: "The offense is defined by the object rather than by an amount: 1347 reaches a scheme against any health care benefit program, which 18 U.S.C. 24(b) defines as any public or private plan or contract affecting commerce, so a commercial insurer is a federal victim, the statute states no dollar figure at all, and the same billing count runs from a 10-year maximum to life where the violation results in serious bodily injury or death."
```

### Why it is false or nonsensical if another charge is swapped in

- Swap in **Medicaid provider fraud, F.S. 409.920**, which is WC-01: the
  assertion becomes false at its centre. That statute is built around a claim
  submitted to the Agency for Health Care Administration, its fiscal agent, or a
  managed care plan. "Any public or private plan or contract affecting commerce"
  is not a description of it. There is no 24(b) on the Florida side and no
  injury-or-death ladder attached to a billing count.
- Swap in **federal money laundering, 18 U.S.C. 1956 and 1957**: false in the
  opposite direction. FED-01's C9 rests on a finite statutory predicate list and
  on a $10,000 figure in 1957. This page rests on a payer definition and on the
  absence of any figure. The two assertions are not neighbours, they are built on
  different kinds of thing.
- Swap in **wire fraud, bank fraud, or any other fraud page**: nonsensical. None
  of them is defined by a class of victim carrying its own statutory definition
  in Title 18's definitions chapter, none of them has the "in connection with the
  delivery of or payment for health care benefits, items, or services" clause,
  and none of them escalates on serious bodily injury or death.

### Collision check performed

`statuteKey` will resolve this page to `18 usc 1347` and WC-01 to `fs 409.920`.
**Different statutes, so `scripts/check-c9.ts` will never compare them.** That is
precisely the failure mode D13 flagged when it said the checker cannot yet hold
this line, so the check was done by hand and is recorded above. Checked by hand
against FED-01 (`18 usc 1956`) and against W2-05 (`fs 896.101`). No collision
with either. **The hand check is the control here; the automated one is not
capable of running on this pair.**
