## 0. THE CONSTRAINT, AND THE VERDICT

**The constraint.** D13 separated this pair by label and description **only**.
Both C9 assertions were left unpopulated, so nothing yet stops the two pages
from being written toward each other. The audit that saved the pair
(OVERLAP-AUDIT.md pair 4) rested on one element difference: 18 U.S.C. 1347
reaches a scheme to defraud **any** health care benefit program, private
insurers included, while F.S. 409.920 is Medicaid specific and is built around
a claim submitted to a named state agency, its contracted fiscal agent, or a
Medicaid managed care plan.

**Binding on this brief: build nothing on private insurers. That is FED-04's
ground.** If this page reaches insurers generally, the element difference that
kept the pair alive disappears and the pair collapses in practice whatever the
audit concluded on paper.

**Verdict: CONSTRAINT SATISFIED, and by a wider margin than expected.** The
Florida statute supplies three separate kinds of substance that have no federal
counterpart at all, all verified this session:

| Limb | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The claim-submission channel | **Satisfied, verified** | 409.920(2)(a)1. names the agency, its fiscal agent, or a managed care plan. "Managed care plan" is a defined term listing seven specific Florida-authorized entity types. There is no analogue in 1347. |
| A dollar ladder the federal statute does not have | **Satisfied, verified** | 409.920(2)(b)1. sets degrees at $10,000 and $50,000. 1347 sets no tiers. |
| Criminal Punishment Code exposure | **Satisfied, verified, and it contains an anomaly** | F.S. 921.0022 ranks the third and second degree tiers at **the same level 7**. The CPC does not apply federally at all, so this limb cannot be reached by FED-04 under any drafting. |

**One additional finding raises the margin further.** The mandatory fine at
409.920(2)(b)3. is five times the pecuniary gain or the Medicaid program's or
managed care organization's loss, whichever is greater, and it is imposed **in
addition to the sentence authorized by law**. Verified.

## 5. The C9 differentiator

Ready to paste into the `c9` field in src/lib/site.ts:

```
statute:   "F.S. 409.920"
section:   "The claim, the ladder, and where the two ladders come apart"
assertion: "The offense is complete when a false statement of material fact reaches a claim submitted to the Agency for Health Care Administration, its fiscal agent, or a Medicaid managed care plan, and its degree ladder at $10,000 and $50,000 comes apart from the F.S. 921.0022 ladder: the third degree tier and the second degree tier are both ranked at level 7, so the statutory maximum triples from 5 years to 15 while the primary offense points stay at 56."
```

### Why it is false or nonsensical if another charge is swapped in

- **Swap in grand theft.** F.S. 812.014 has no claim, no agency, and no fiscal
  agent. Its levels climb with value across a much longer ladder. Every clause
  is false.
- **Swap in the Florida money laundering charge.** F.S. 896.101 tiers at $300,
  $20,000, and $100,000 across any 12-month period and its levels climb 7, 8,
  9 in step with the degrees. **The anomaly asserted here is precisely what
  896.101 does not have**, so the assertion is not merely false there, it is the
  opposite of true.
- **Swap in the federal counterpart.** 18 U.S.C. 1347 has no Florida agency, no
  dollar tiers, and no Criminal Punishment Code at all. The second half of the
  assertion is not false there so much as inapplicable, which is the stronger
  form of the test.

### Pairwise separation from FED-04, stated so it can be enforced

D13 left both assertions unpopulated and said the checker cannot hold this line
until they are filled. This one is now fillable. **FED-04's assertion must not
contain any of: the Agency for Health Care Administration, a fiscal agent, a
Medicaid managed care plan, F.S. 921.0022, an offense level, or a scoresheet
point value.** None of those exist federally, so a FED-04 assertion containing
them would be wrong on the merits as well as colliding.

Conversely, **this assertion contains nothing about private insurers, health
care benefit programs generally, federal loss tables, or federal sentencing**,
which is the ground the audit reserved for FED-04.

### Automated collision check

`statuteKey` resolves this to `fs 409.920`. No other charge in src/lib/site.ts
rests on 409.920, so the checker will not compare it against anything. **That is
exactly why the hand check above matters more than the automated one**, and it
is the same situation the FED-01 brief recorded for the money laundering pair.

## 6. Sources and verification status

### Verified this session, fetched 2026-08-13

**https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/409.920** (allowlisted:
flsenate.gov). 2025 Florida Statutes.

Section catchline, quoted:
> "Medicaid provider fraud."

Prohibited acts, 409.920(2)(a), quoted:
> "A person may not: 1. Knowingly make, cause to be made, or aid and abet in the making of any false statement or false representation of a material fact, by commission or omission, in any claim submitted to the agency or its fiscal agent or a managed care plan for payment. 2. Knowingly make, cause to be made, or aid and abet in the making of a claim for items or services that are not authorized to be reimbursed by the Medicaid program. 3. Knowingly charge, solicit, accept, or receive anything of value, other than an authorized copayment from a Medicaid recipient, from any source in addition to the amount legally payable for an item or service provided to a Medicaid recipient under the Medicaid program or knowingly fail to credit the agency or its fiscal agent for any payment received from a third-party source. 4. Knowingly make or in any way cause to be made any false statement or false representation of a material fact, by commission or omission, in any document containing items of income and expense that is or may be used by the agency to determine a general or specific rate of payment for an item or service provided by a provider. 5. Knowingly solicit, offer, pay, or receive any remuneration, including any kickback, bribe, or rebate, directly or indirectly, overtly or covertly, in cash or in kind, in return for referring an individual to a person for the furnishing or arranging for the furnishing of any item or service for which payment may be made, in whole or in part, under the Medicaid program, or in return for obtaining, purchasing, leasing, ordering, or arranging for or recommending, obtaining, purchasing, leasing, or ordering any goods, facility, item, or service, for which payment may be made, in whole or in part, under the Medicaid program. This subparagraph does not apply to any discount, payment, waiver of payment, or payment practice not prohibited by 42 U.S.C. s. 1320a-7b(b) or any regulations adopted thereunder. 6. Knowingly submit false or misleading information or statements to the Medicaid program for the purpose of being accepted as a Medicaid provider. 7. Knowingly use or endeavor to use a Medicaid provider's identification number or a Medicaid recipient's identification number to make, cause to be made, or aid and abet in the making of a claim for items or services that are not authorized to be reimbursed by the Medicaid program."

Penalties, 409.920(2)(b)1. and 2., quoted:
> "1. A person who violates this subsection and receives or endeavors to receive anything of value of: a. Ten thousand dollars or less commits a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084. b. More than $10,000, but less than $50,000, commits a felony of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084. c. Fifty thousand dollars or more commits a felony of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.
> 2. The value of separate funds, goods, or services that a person received or attempted to receive pursuant to a scheme or course of conduct may be aggregated in determining the degree of the offense."

Mandatory fine, 409.920(2)(b)3., quoted:
> "In addition to the sentence authorized by law, a person who is convicted of a violation of this subsection shall pay a fine in an amount equal to five times the pecuniary gain unlawfully received or the loss incurred by the Medicaid program or managed care organization, whichever is greater."

Definitions, 409.920(1), quoted:
> "Agency" means "the Agency for Health Care Administration".
> "Fiscal agent" means "any individual, firm, corporation, partnership, organization, or other legal entity that has contracted with the agency to receive, process, and adjudicate claims under the Medicaid program."
> "Managed care plans" means "a health insurer authorized under chapter 624, an exclusive provider organization authorized under chapter 627, a health maintenance organization authorized under chapter 641, a prepaid health plan authorized under this chapter, a provider service network authorized under this chapter, a minority physician network authorized under this chapter, and an emergency department diversion program authorized under this chapter or the General Appropriations Act, providing health care services pursuant to a contract with the Medicaid program."
> "Knowingly" means "the act was done voluntarily and intentionally and not because of mistake or accident", including willful conduct with "specific intent to do something that the law forbids".

Enforcement, 409.920(9), quoted:
> "The Attorney General shall conduct a statewide program of Medicaid fraud control."

Civil remedies, 409.920(10)(d), quoted:
> "Seek any civil remedy provided by law, including, but not limited to, the remedies provided in ss. 68.081-68.092 and 812.035 and this chapter."

### The combined finding, stated once so the page does not have to derive it

The **lowest** tier of this offence, $10,000 or less, is a third degree felony
that scores **56 primary offense points**, which is **above the 44-point
threshold** at which the lowest permissible sentence is a nonstate prison
sanction, before any other scoresheet category is added. verified from
921.0022, 921.0024, and 409.920 together. The second tier triples the statutory
maximum from 5 years to 15 and adds **zero** primary offense points.

**This is the strongest verified fact in the brief and no page in the observed
top ten carries it.** It is also the reason the C9 section is the table rather
than the elements: the elements are what the charge is, but the table is what
the reader did not know.
