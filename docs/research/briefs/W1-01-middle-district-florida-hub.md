### Allowlist gap: RESOLVED 2026-08-13 (DECISIONS.md D2)

The gap this brief raised was real: Section 10.3 allowlisted "uscourts.gov and
ussc.gov" for "federal statutes and guidelines," and **neither publishes the
text of the United States Code**, which made C12 unsatisfiable for every federal
statutory claim across seven of the ten briefs.

**Amended.** uscode.house.gov is allowlisted for U.S. Code text. justice.gov is
allowlisted **for procedure and DOJ policy only, and explicitly not as a source
for what a statute says**, because the Justice Manual is charging policy rather
than law and does not bind a court.

**Two consequences for this page specifically.** The Speedy Trial Act and 18
U.S.C. 3142 items in the table above are now fetchable rather than blocked. And
PAA #1, the current U.S. Attorney for the Middle District, is answerable from
justice.gov/usao-mdfl, which falls squarely inside the procedure-and-policy
scope rather than the excluded one.
