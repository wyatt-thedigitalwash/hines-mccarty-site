## 3. The question set a person would ask an AI assistant

1. "How many grams of cocaine is trafficking in Florida?"
2. "Is there a minimum sentence for drug trafficking in Florida?"
3. "Can a judge go below the minimum mandatory in a Florida trafficking case?"
4. "I only had it for personal use. Why am I charged with trafficking?"
5. "Is my case state or federal?"
6. "What is the difference between trafficking and possession with intent to
   sell?"
7. "Does the weight include the pills themselves or just the drug?"
8. "What happens if a gun was in the car?"

Questions 1, 2, and 3 are the FAQ block. Question 4 is the most important
paragraph on the page and the one competitors handle worst: Florida trafficking
turns on weight, not on intent to sell, so a personal-use quantity above a
threshold is still trafficking. Question 7 is the mixture question and is
genuinely charge-specific. Question 8 is the 10-20-Life link, which is verified
below and is a real cross-charge fact.

## 5. The C9 differentiator, named

**Cover the words "drug trafficking" and the page is still identifiable,
because Section 3 is a table of substance-specific weights, each tied to a
mandatory minimum term and a fixed fine, and no other statute in Florida
produces that structure.**

Section 7 passes independently: 10-20-Life's enumerated list names trafficking
in cannabis, cocaine and illegal drugs specifically, so that section is false
for every non-trafficking charge in the silo.

## 6. Sources and verification status

### Verified this session, fetched 2026-08-13

**https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/893.135** (allowlisted:
flsenate.gov). 2025 Florida Statutes.

> (1) opening: "Except as authorized in this chapter or in chapter 499 and notwithstanding the provisions of s. 893.13"

Cannabis, (1)(a):
> 1.: "Is in excess of 25 pounds, but less than 2,000 pounds ... sentenced to a mandatory minimum term of imprisonment of 3 years, and ... pay a fine of $25,000."
> 2.: "Is 2,000 pounds or more, but less than 10,000 pounds ... sentenced to a mandatory minimum term of imprisonment of 7 years, and ... pay a fine of $50,000."
> 3.: "Is 10,000 pounds or more ... sentenced to a mandatory minimum term of imprisonment of 15 calendar years and pay a fine of $200,000."

Cocaine, (1)(b)1.:
> a.: "Is 28 grams or more, but less than 200 grams ... sentenced to a mandatory minimum term of imprisonment of 3 years, and ... pay a fine of $50,000."
> b.: "Is 200 grams or more, but less than 400 grams ... sentenced to a mandatory minimum term of imprisonment of 7 years, and ... pay a fine of $100,000."
> c.: "Is 400 grams or more, but less than 150 kilograms ... sentenced to a mandatory minimum term of imprisonment of 15 calendar years and pay a fine of $250,000."

Degree, throughout subsection (1):
> "Commits a felony of the first degree"

**The finding inside that table.** The lowest trafficking tier for any substance
is offense level 7. Every trafficking charge in Florida therefore starts at a
level that scores above the 44-point nonstate-prison line before any prior
record, victim injury, or multiplier is added. That is verified from the chart
and it is the single clearest way to explain to a reader why trafficking is
different from every other drug charge.

**https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/775.087** (allowlisted), 2025.
The 10-20-Life enumerated list at (2)(a)1. includes, verbatim at sub-subparagraph p.:
> "Trafficking in cannabis, trafficking in cocaine, capital importation of cocaine, trafficking in illegal drugs, capital importation of illegal ..."

and the operative minimum terms:
> "shall be sentenced to a minimum term of imprisonment of 10 years" (possession of a firearm during an enumerated felony)
> "shall be sentenced to a minimum term of imprisonment of 20 years" (discharge)
> "not less than 25 years and not more than a term of imprisonment of life in prison" (discharge causing death or great bodily harm)

**https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/921.0024** (allowlisted), 2025.
The scoresheet worksheet includes a multipliers section, and **drug trafficking
is one of the named multipliers**. verified from the fetched section structure.
The multiplier's numeric value was **not** captured and is UNVERIFIED, below.
