## 5. The C9 differentiator

Ready to paste into the `c9` field in `src/lib/site.ts`:

```
statute:   "18 U.S.C. 1344"
section:   "What the government has to prove: the institution is the element"
assertion: "There is no mailing and no wire in this statute; the financial institution defined at 18 U.S.C. 20 is itself the element, and it carries a $1,000,000 fine and a 30-year maximum as the baseline rather than as an enhancement to a lower one."
```

### Why it is false or nonsensical with another charge swapped in

| Swap in | Result |
|---|---|
| **Wire fraud (18 U.S.C. 1343)** | **False on both halves.** 1343 has a wire element by name, and its baseline is "fined under this title or imprisoned not more than 20 years." The $1,000,000 and 30 years arrive only through the clause "or affects a financial institution," which is precisely the enhancement this assertion says 1344 does not need. Verified on both sides. |
| **Mail fraud (18 U.S.C. 1341)** | **False.** A mailing is the element. This is also the pair Section 4.6.1 collapsed into wire fraud, so no page can claim it. |
| **Federal money laundering (18 U.S.C. 1956, 1957)** | **False.** 1956 has no financial institution element at all. 1957 does involve a monetary transaction through a financial institution, but it is gated at "greater than $10,000" and capped at ten years, so both halves of the assertion fail: there is a threshold and the maximum is a third of this one. |
| **Healthcare fraud (18 U.S.C. 1347)** | **False.** The object is a health care benefit program, not a financial institution. |
| **Any Florida charge** | **Nonsensical.** No Florida statute defines a financial institution as the element of a fraud offence, and Florida fraud offences are graded by value under F.S. 921.0022, which this charge has no row in. |

**The PPP adjacency, and how it is resolved.** OPP-01's C9 rests on a limitations
period. This brief has now **verified** the limitations statute that OPP-01
records as its publish blocker: 18 U.S.C. 3293 sets ten years for violations of,
among others, sections 1014 and 1344, and for 1341 or 1343 where the offence
affects a financial institution, against the general five years at 18 U.S.C.
3282. Quoted in Section 6.

Two consequences, and both should be actioned rather than left implicit.

1. **The bank fraud C9 does not use the limitations period.** It is available,
   it is verified, and it is deliberately left out of the assertion so that the
   substance belongs to one page. The ten-year period appears on this page only
   as **FAQ question 7**, which is a two-sentence answer, not a section.
2. **OPP-01 should be revisited with 3293 in hand.** Its publish blocker was
   "verify the operative limitations statute." 3293 is now fetched and quoted.
   Note carefully what it does and does not do for that page: it covers 1344 and
   1014 outright, and covers 1341 and 1343 **only where the offence affects a
   financial institution**. A PPP count charged as bare wire fraud with no bank
   involved is not obviously inside 3293. That is a distinction OPP-01 does not
   currently draw, and it is a better differentiator for that page than the one
   it has. **This is a note for that brief's second pass, not a change made here.**

### Verified this session, fetched 2026-08-13, uscode.house.gov (allowlisted per DECISIONS.md D2)

**https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1344&num=0&edition=prelim**
Currency note on the page: "Text contains those laws in effect on August 12,
2026." Complete section, quoted:

> §1344. Bank fraud
>
> Whoever knowingly executes, or attempts to execute, a scheme or artifice--
>
> (1) to defraud a financial institution; or
>
> (2) to obtain any of the moneys, funds, credits, assets, securities, or other property owned by, or under the custody or control of, a financial institution, by means of false or fraudulent pretenses, representations, or promises;
>
> shall be fined not more than $1,000,000 or imprisoned not more than 30 years, or both.

Source credit and amendment history, as returned: added by Pub. L. 98-473, title
II, §1108(a), Oct. 12, 1984; amended by Pub. L. 101-73, title IX, §961(k), Aug.
9, 1989; amended by Pub. L. 101-647, title XXV, §2504(j), Nov. 29, 1990, which
raised the maximum term from 20 years to 30.

**Three observations from that text, all load-bearing.**
- The section contains **no dollar threshold, no tier, and no degree**. That is
  an absence read directly off the complete quoted section, not an inference.
- **"or attempts to execute"** is on the face of the statute and inside the same
  sentence as the penalty. Constraint B's opening move rests on this.
- Neither limb contains a mailing, a wire, a transmission, or any interstate
  commerce phrase. The direction's premise is confirmed from the text.
